Conclusions: (1) dominant in-band distortion = edge-aligned (single-edge) uniform-sampled PWM 2nd harmonic,
centre-aligned removes ~30 dB; (2) 2f-48k fold of 14-20k inputs is removed by real x4 interpolation;
(3) quantization (4160 counts) is NOT the limit at -6 dBFS (A0~A1); HREN matters only at low level;
(4) CK-only: elastic linear interp makes ASRC-like moving sidebands -> likely what was heard on CK.
